The Restaurant

A kitchen built around fire and patience

Our restaurant menu runs on two things: a 450°C wood-fired oven and sauces that simmer for hours before they touch a plate. From hand-stretched pizza dough to pasta finished table-side in flavor, if not in ceremony — every dish is built to order, never reheated.

  • Wood-fired pizzas, stretched by hand daily
  • Slow-braised pasta sauces, simmered for hours
  • Chargrilled burgers on brioche, made to order

The Bakery

Laminated, proofed, and baked before sunrise

Our bakers start at 4am so the first croissant comes out golden by opening time. Butter-laminated pastries, naturally leavened sourdough, and celebration cakes built layer by layer — nothing here is mass-produced, and nothing sits under a heat lamp.

  • 36-hour laminated croissants and pastries
  • Naturally leavened sourdough, 48-hour ferment
  • Custom celebration cakes with cake customizer

Our Story

Two crafts, one kitchen

Kitchen Paradise started in 2014 as a single wood-fired oven and a question: why should a bakery and a restaurant be separate businesses when they share the same values? Fresh ingredients, sourced daily. Chefs and bakers who trained under people who cared. No shortcuts, no reheated plates, no pastry that sat overnight. What began as a single storefront has grown into a full kitchen serving both crafts — but the standard hasn't moved an inch.
